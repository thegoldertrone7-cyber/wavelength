Performance & motion

01 — Feel

Navigation shipped

Frame-ready page changes

Replace the blocking route pause and expensive full-page blur with a shorter, GPU-friendly fade and lift. Same rhythm, less waiting.

Visualizer shipped

Adaptive visualizer loop

The canvas runs only when visible, pauses in the background, and backs off to an efficient refresh rate while keeping the motion fluid.

Playback UI shipped

Cheaper progress updates

Progress, clocks, and media-session state update once per animation frame with cached nodes instead of forcing repeated DOM scans.

Compositing shipped

Motion on the right layer

Animate transform and opacity where possible, isolate long lists, and remove paint-heavy effects from active animations without changing their appearance.

Interaction shipped

Springier, quieter micro-motion

Unify easing and timing across views, rows, cards, toasts, menus, and modals. Less jitter; more intentional movement.

Accessibility shipped

Real motion control

Respect the system preference automatically and add an in-app Motion setting for people who want reduced movement.

Images shipped

Smarter artwork loading

Decode covers asynchronously, reserve layout space, and fade them in once ready to prevent flashes and layout shifts.

Idle work shipped

Pause what isn’t visible

Stop visualizer, polling, and nonessential animation work when the window is hidden or the full-screen player is closed.

Lossless & quality

03 — Source

Local library shipped

Your own files, untouched

Add FLAC, WAV, AIFF, ALAC, M4A, MP3, OGG, or Opus files. Wavelength copies them into its data folder, reads their metadata and embedded artwork with ffprobe/ffmpeg, and keeps them available offline.

Native playback shipped

No transcoding

Lossless local files play at their original bit depth and sample rate through range-enabled streaming. The interface shows the exact format, bit depth, and sample rate.

File actions shipped

Add, rescan, remove

A dedicated Local files view with add-files, folder rescan, delete, storage totals, and a clear library count. Files can also be managed from Settings.

Streaming shipped

Best available or data saver

Choose the highest-bitrate YouTube stream or a lower-bandwidth option. Resolutions are cached per quality so switching does not repeat the slow extraction step.

Clarity shipped

Honest quality labels

YouTube-sourced audio remains labeled as lossy. Lossless badges appear only for local files that genuinely retain their original quality.

07 — Together

Search shipped

A starting point, not a blank page

Search now opens with recent searches, genre shortcuts, and a focused query field. Results lead with the strongest match and keep the rest scannable below.

Results shipped

One clear top result

Play the best match in one click, see the result count before scrolling, and clear the query without losing your place in the app.

Theme, streaming quality, and device sync appear at the top. A filter and section jump bar make a long settings page quick to navigate.
